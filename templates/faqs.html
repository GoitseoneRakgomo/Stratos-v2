OVERVIEW

General Questions

What corporate services does Stratos provide?

We support business formation, accounting and bookkeeping, auditing, VAT registration, residency and visas, bank account opening, Golden Visas, and ongoing corporate compliance across the UAE.

How do I choose the right business structure?

We consider your business activity, ownership structure, target customers, visa requirements, banking needs, and long-term objectives before recommending the most suitable setup (Free Zone, Mainland, or Offshore).

Can my UAE business be established remotely?

Many steps can be completed remotely through our streamlined digital processes. Government and banking procedures may require in-person attendance, which we coordinate and assist you with on-site.

How does dedicated Relationship Manager support work?

From your initial consultation, you receive a dedicated Relationship Manager who understands your goals, acts as your single point of contact, and coordinates execution at every step.

STAGE 1 PROTOCOL

Client Onboarding & Documentation

At Stratos Consulting, we begin every engagement with a structured onboarding process to ensure efficiency, compliance, and personalized service. Below is a detailed outline of what’s required and what you can expect in the first stage of your business setup journey.

Step 1: Information & Document Collection (Individual Shareholders)

To initiate the setup process, please provide the following documentation:

  • Three Preferred Company Names: Listed in clear order of preference for reservation.
  • Business Activities: Up to 3 activities allowed depending on licensing authority guidelines.
  • Full-Color Passport Copy: Must be high-resolution and valid for at least 7 months.
  • Passport Photograph: Recent, high-resolution digital photo with a plain background.
  • Personal Bank Statements: Most recent 6 months, issued in English.
  • Proof of Residential Address: Utility bill or official government document issued within the last 3 months.

Our compliance team will review all documents to ensure accuracy and completeness prior to authority submission.

Step 2: Client Onboarding Workflow
  1. Introduction Call: We schedule a welcome briefing to introduce your dedicated team, verify your objectives, and align operational preferences.
  2. Proposal & Engagement Agreement: You will receive a customized proposal outlining the scope of services, completion timelines, transparent fee breakdown, and terms. Service commences upon agreement execution.
  3. Information Confirmation: Our advisors walk you through final clarifications, finalize corporate activity selections, and prepare authority filings.

TERMS & COMPLIANCE

Government Fees, Approvals & Limitations

Government Fees Protocol

All official government charges are supported by official payment vouchers. Government fees are payable upfront prior to filing and must be settled in full before submission of documentation to relevant authorities.

Activity Approvals & Free Zone Authority Review

All selected business activities are subject to final approval by the relevant licensing authority or Free Zone. In the event an activity requires external regulatory approval or amendment, our team provides alternative activity mapping and coordinates filings seamlessly.

Shareholder Limitations & Establishment Cards

Standard setup structures accommodate up to three (3) shareholders. Supplementary administrative fees apply for additional corporate or individual shareholders. Establishment cards are issued specifically for entity structures with visa allocations.

Proposal Validity

Engagement proposals are valid for a period of 30 days from issuance, after which government fees, regulatory terms, or activity availability may be subject to authority adjustments.

Process & Project Timeline

Step 1: Initial Setup & Onboarding Approx. 48 Hours

Invoice settlement, engagement agreement execution, document verification, and immediate filing initiation with licensing authorities.

In-person arrival in Dubai, VIP escorted Medical Fitness examination, biometrics capture, and official Emirates ID & Residency Visa stamping.
