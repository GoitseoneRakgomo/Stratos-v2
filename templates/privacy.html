Privacy Policy

How Stratos handles information shared through our website and services.

Welcome to Stratos

Welcome to www.stratosuae.com. Stratos Consulting LLC FZCO (“Stratos,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal data responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data (“ UAE PDPL”), the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the United Kingdom General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018 as amended by the California Consumer Privacy Act or California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA), and other applicable data protection laws.

Stratos Consulting LLC FZCO acts as a data controller in respect of personal data processed through our website and in the course of providing our services. We process personal data lawfully, fairly, and transparently in accordance with the principles of purpose limitation, data minimisation, storage limitation, accuracy, integrity, and confidentiality. This Policy is tailored to our operations as a UAE-based corporate advisory and setup consultancy. Our processing of personal data reflects the staged nature of our services which includes handling client enquiries, onboarding, Know-Your-Customer (KYC) and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) compliance processes, regulatory filings, and ongoing client support services.

We do not represent that all provisions of GDPR, UK GDPR, or CCPA/CPRA apply in every case. These frameworks are referenced only to the extent that they are applicable to a particular data subject, transaction, or jurisdiction.

Definitions

For purposes of this Privacy Policy, the following definitions apply:

  • “Personal Data” means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, including identifiers such as name, email address, IP address, or any other data that can directly or indirectly identify an individual.
  • “Processing” means any operation or set of operations performed on personal data, whether or not by automated means, including collection, recording, organisation, structuring, storage, adaptation, retrieval, consultation, use, disclosure, restriction, erasure, or destruction.
  • “Data Subject” means any identified or identifiable natural person whose personal data is processed by us.
  • “Controller” means the entity which determines the purposes and means of the processing of personal data. For the purposes of this Privacy Policy, Stratos Consulting LLC FZCO acts as the Controller.
  • “Processor” means a third party that processes personal data on behalf of the Controller.
  • “Cookies” means small text files placed on your device to store information about your browsing activity and preferences.
  • “Consent” means any freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous indication of the Data Subject’s wishes by which they signify agreement to the processing of personal data.
  • “Third Party” means any natural or legal person, public authority, agency, or body other than the Data Subject, Controller, or Processor.
  • “Applicable Laws” means all data protection and privacy laws that may apply to the processing of personal data, including UAE PDPL, GDPR, UK GDPR, and CCPA/CPRA.

Website Updates and Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy and other website policies from time to time to reflect changes in legal, technical, or business developments. Any updates will take effect upon publication on our website.

Where required by applicable law, we will notify users of material changes through appropriate means, including email notifications or prominent notices on our website.

Who We Are

Our official website is www.stratosuae.com.

The legal operating entity is Stratos Consulting LLC FZCO. We provide corporate setup, licensing, residency, banking, tax, and compliance advisory services in the UAE. For the purposes of applicable data protection laws, we act as the data controller of your personal information. However, depending on the nature of the engagement, Stratos may also act as an intermediary or facilitator in transmitting personal data to third parties (including banks, Free Zones, regulators, and professional advisors), each of whom may act as independent data controllers.

Information We Collect

We collect and process personal data based on how you interact with Stratos, typically across the following stages:

  • Enquiries and Initial Contact: We collect information directly from you when you register an account, contact us, submit enquiries, leave comments, or use our services. This may include your name, email address, telephone number, postal address, and any other information you choose to provide. This is used to respond to your request and assess potential engagement.
  • Onboarding and Client Services: During client onboarding and delivery of services, we collect additional information including postal address, identification documents, date and place of birth, nationality, professional or employment-related details, source of funds or wealth information, tax identification numbers, background and risk assessment information where required by financial institutions or regulators, as well as any additional documentation required by banks, Free Zones, or government authorities.
  • KYC and Regulatory Compliance: As part of mandatory Know-Your-Customer (KYC), Anti-Money Laundering (AML), and other regulatory requirements, we collect and verify identification, beneficial ownership, politically exposed person (PEP) status, sources of funds and wealth, sanctions screening data and related due diligence data. This data is processed primarily to comply with UAE regulatory obligations and to facilitate introductions to banks or Free Zones.
  • Marketing and Communications: We may collect contact details for sending service updates, newsletters, relevant marketing communication or promotional materials where you have consented or where permitted by law. You may opt out at any time using the unsubscribe link or by contacting us.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for a lawful purpose and subject to appropriate safeguards or consent where required by law.

Failure to provide the mandatory data may prevent us from onboarding or continuing to act for you.

Comments and Cookies

We use essential cookies and similar technologies to ensure the proper functioning of our website and improve user experience. These help us remember your preferences or analyse site usage to improve our services. We place non-essential cookies, for analytics or marketing, only with your consent where required by law, including under the GDPR and UK GDPR.

You manage your cookie preferences through our cookie banner or your browser settings. We do not use cookies for public comment features or login persistence on this website.

Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR and UK GDPR)

We process your personal data on the following legal bases:

  • We process data where it is necessary for the performance of a contract with you or to take steps prior to entering into a contract.
  • We process data where it is necessary for our legitimate business interests, including improving our services, ensuring website security, and preventing fraud, provided that such interests are not overridden by your rights and freedoms.
  • We process data where we have obtained your consent, particularly for marketing communications and non-essential cookies.
  • We also process personal data where it is necessary to comply with legal obligations.

Sharing of Personal Information

We may share your personal data only where necessary and with appropriate safeguards with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our website and delivering services. These include:

  • Hosting providers, analytics tools, email service providers, our enterprise system known as Odoo ERP, licensed professional partners for legal, accounting, or banking facilitation, and technology vendors. These providers are bound by data processing agreements. These agreements require them to process data only on our instructions, maintain confidentiality, and comply with applicable laws.
  • Regulatory and legal authorities. These include UAE regulators, law enforcement, courts, or other authorities where required by law. Examples include licensing approvals, Know-Your-Customer (KYC) and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) reporting.
  • Our professional advisors such as legal or accounting advisors for service delivery or compliance purposes. We do this under strict confidentiality obligations.

We do not sell your personal information for monetary consideration. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising as defined under California law unless explicitly disclosed and consented to where required.

Due to the nature of our services, personal data may be transferred to jurisdictions outside your country of residence, including:

  • Transfers within the UAE; and
  • Transfers to financial institutions, regulators, and business partners in other jurisdictions.

Where such transfers occur, we rely on appropriate safeguards, including contractual protections, as well as transfers that are necessary for the performance of a contract or required to comply with applicable legal obligations.

Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as is necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including to provide services, comply with legal, regulatory (including AML/KYC requirements), accounting, dispute resolution, enforcement of agreements, and reporting requirements.

Comments and related metadata may be retained indefinitely to facilitate automatic recognition and approval of follow-up comments.

User account information is retained for as long as the account remains active and thereafter as required for legal or legitimate business purposes. Users may request deletion of their accounts, subject to legal retention obligations.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including role-based access controls, secure storage environments, encryption and secure transmission protocols, internal confidentiality and access restrictions, and enhanced protection for compliance-related data. Personal data collected for business and marketing purposes is stored securely within our Odoo system and related infrastructure.

Where required by law, we will notify relevant supervisory authorities and affected individuals in the event of a personal data breach. Despite the aforementioned measures, no system is completely secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

Your rights depend on applicable law. We verify your identity before processing requests and respond within legal timeframes. Your common rights include the following:

  • You may request access to your personal data and obtain a copy of the information we hold about you.
  • You may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • You may request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal and regulatory obligations.
  • You may object to or request restriction of certain processing activities, including processing for marketing purposes.
  • You may withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.

If you are a California resident, you may also have the right to request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information collected, request deletion of personal information, opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and exercise your rights without discrimination.

We will respond to verified requests within the timeframes required by applicable law. We will verify your identity before processing requests, respond within applicable legal timeframes, and inform you where a request cannot be fulfilled and the reasons why.

If you have concerns about how your personal data is handled, you may contact us directly.

Where applicable, you may also lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority in your jurisdiction.

Our services are not intended for individuals under the age of 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

To exercise your rights, please contact us at info@stratosuae.com.

Your personal data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your jurisdiction, including countries that may not provide the same level of data protection.

Where such transfers occur, we will implement appropriate safeguards in accordance with applicable laws, including standard contractual clauses, adequacy decisions, or other lawful transfer mechanisms recognised under GDPR, UK GDPR, UAE PDPL, and CCPA.
