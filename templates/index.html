The Story Behind
STRATOS

Stratos Consulting was founded to solve a problem that serious founders, investors, and operators repeatedly face when entering the UAE: a fragmented, opaque, and compliance-heavy system that is poorly explained and badly coordinated.

After years working in corporate advisory, finance, and international business development, the founding team saw a clear pattern. Too many entrepreneurs were being:

  • misguided into unsuitable structures
  • delayed by incomplete documentation
  • or rejected by banks and authorities due to poor preparation

Stratos was created to replace that with a structured, compliant, and accountable approach to market entry in the UAE.
